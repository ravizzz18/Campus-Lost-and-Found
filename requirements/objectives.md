
# Project Objectives

1. Develop a centralized platform for managing lost and found items within a college campus.

2. Allow students and authorized staff to report lost belongings with relevant descriptions, categories, dates and locations.

3. Enable users to register found items and provide information that can help identify their owners.

4. Provide search and filtering facilities to locate relevant lost and found records.

5. Implement an ownership claim mechanism to allow users to request the recovery of their belongings.

6. Provide administrative facilities to review reports, verify claims and update item statuses.

7. Maintain organized records of reported items and their recovery progress.

8. Improve communication and coordination between students, staff and campus administrators.
