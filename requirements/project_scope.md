
# Project Scope

## In Scope

- Student and administrator authentication.
- User profile management.
- Reporting lost items.
- Reporting found items.
- Uploading item photographs.
- Searching and filtering item records.
- Viewing item details.
- Submitting ownership claims.
- Administrative verification of claims.
- Updating item recovery status.
- Maintaining records of reported and recovered items.

## Out of Scope

- Integration with external college ERP systems.
- Physical delivery of recovered belongings.
- Payment processing.
- Automatic identification through advanced AI models.
- Integration with external lost and found platforms.

## Assumptions

- Users have access to an internet-connected device.
- Students and staff provide reasonably accurate information.
- Administrators are responsible for reviewing ownership claims.
- The system is initially intended for a single college campus.

## Limitations

- The accuracy of reports depends on user-provided information.
- Ownership verification requires administrative intervention.
- The prototype may initially operate in a local development environment.
