
# Problem Statement

In college campuses, students and staff frequently lose personal belongings such as identity cards, books, wallets, electronic devices, keys and other essential items.

Currently, the process of reporting and recovering lost belongings often depends on manual communication, physical inquiries, notice boards or informal messaging groups. These approaches make it difficult to maintain organized records, search for reported items and verify ownership.

The absence of a centralized platform can result in delayed recovery, misplaced information, duplicate reports and difficulty coordinating between individuals who lose items and those who find them.

Therefore, a Campus Lost and Found Management System is proposed to provide a centralized web-based platform where students and authorized college staff can report lost and found items, search available records, submit ownership claims and track the recovery process.

The system will also provide administrative facilities to review reports, verify ownership claims and manage the status of reported items.
